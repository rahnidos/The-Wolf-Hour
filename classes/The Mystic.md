# # The Mystic

While others cling to cold steel and logic, the Mystic peers into the shadows and the light. They are the keepers of the spirit in an age of rust, seeking to understand the metaphysical forces that have shaped the Wolf Hour.

## Subclasses

### The Night-Seeker

The Night-Seeker is a scholar of the darkness. They study the origins of the "Night," the nature of the monsters that haunt the void, and the ancient rituals that might hold them at bay. They are sensitive to the presence of malevolent forces and act as the first line of defense against the encroaching corruption.

### The Dawn-Bringer

The Dawn-Bringer is a beacon of hope and prophecy. They lead the weary through the darkness, preaching of a future "Dawn" where humanity will reclaim the stars. Some possess the rare gift of precognition, seeing flashes of what is to come and guiding their followers away from impending disaster.

## Core Ability

**Third Eye:** Once per session, a Mystic can sense the spiritual or metaphysical "temperature" of a location, detecting lingering traces of the Night or receiving a cryptic omen about a future event.
