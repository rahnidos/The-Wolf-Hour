For a Voidfarer, a spaceship is not just a machine, but a living sanctuary. They are the masters of the "Clean Routes" and the only ones capable of dancing between the stars without being swallowed by the silence of the Night.

## Subclasses

### The Pilot

The Pilot is an extension of the ship’s thrusters and hull. They focus on manual flight, combat maneuvers, and the sheer intuition required to pull a multi-ton vessel through an asteroid field or atmospheric storm. If it can fly, the Pilot can push it to its absolute limit.
### The Astrogator

The Astrogator is the mind of the ship. They manage the complex calculations of Fold-travel, long-range sensor arrays, and the distribution of power between life support and engines. They are trained to find safe paths through corrupted sectors and keep the ship's internal systems humming in perfect synchronization.

## Core Ability

**Sync-Link:** A Voidfarer can "read" a ship's status without looking at the monitors. They gain a bonus to any roll involving ship operations or identifying anomalies in nearby space.
