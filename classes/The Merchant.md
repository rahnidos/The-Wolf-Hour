# The Merchant

Where others see ruins and danger, the Merchant sees profit and opportunity. They are the masters of barter, the bridge between isolated systems, and sometimes the architects of a community's financial ruin.

## Subclasses

### The Peddler

The Peddler is a nomadic merchant who spends their life in the cockpit of a freighter. They specialize in logistics, knowing exactly where a spare atmospheric filter is worth its weight in fuel. They are experts at finding "Clean Routes" and maintaining a network of contacts across the void. To a Peddler, the journey is just a series of transactions.

### The Swindler

The Swindler views the naivety of rebuilding societies as a resource to be harvested. They are experts at selling "Old World relics" that are actually junk, fencing stolen goods, and using charisma to talk people out of their life savings. They operate in the shadows of the markets, always ready to disappear before their marks realize they've been cheated.

## Core Ability

**Market Appraisal:** A Merchant can instantly determine the true value of any item and knows exactly who in the current sector would be most desperate to buy it or what they would be willing to trade for it.
