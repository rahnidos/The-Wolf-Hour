# The Warden

In the Wolf Hour, law is a memory and society is a fragile dream. The Warden is the one who steps forward to organize the survivors, establishing the rules and bonds necessary for humanity to endure another day in the dark.

## Subclasses

### The Marshal

The Marshal is the shield and the gavel of a community. They focus on maintaining internal order, managing resources, and ensuring that everyone follows the local code. Part administrator and part lawman, they know how to spot a troublemaker and how to organize a local militia to defend their home against outside threats.

### The Envoy

The Envoy is the voice of reason and the architect of alliances. They specialize in de-escalating conflicts before blood is shed and building lasting connections between isolated groups. As a natural commander, they inspire others to work together, turning a group of strangers into a cohesive team through charisma and negotiation.

## Core Ability

**Authority's Presence:** A Warden can command the attention of a room. Once per session, they can automatically de-escalate a non-violent confrontation or force a group of NPCs to listen to a single, reasonable request.
