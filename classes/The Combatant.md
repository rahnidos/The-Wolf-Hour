# The Combatant
In the Wolf Hour, violence is often the first and last line of defense. The Combatant is a survivor who has mastered the tools of war, whether to protect a flickering outpost or to take what they need from the ruins of others.

## Subclasses

### The Marine

The Marine is a powerhouse of the battlefield, specializing in direct confrontation and overwhelming fire superiority. They are trained to wear heavy boarding suits and wield massive weaponry that would break a lesser person's shoulder. When a door needs to be kicked down or a corridor held against a swarm of feral bots, the Marine is the first one in.

### The Ranger

The Ranger is a master of the environment and the unseen strike. They specialize in camouflage, reconnaissance, and precision. A Ranger doesn't want a fair fight; they prefer to strike from the shadows, sabotage enemy equipment, and vanish before the smoke clears. They are the eyes and ears of any crew venturing into unknown territory.

## Core Ability

**Combat Reflexes:** A Combatant can act instantly at the start of any hostile encounter, gaining a bonus to their first attack or movement before anyone else can react.
