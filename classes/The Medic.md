# The Medic

A Medic in the Wolf Hour is more than a doctor; they are a keeper of the human flame. They manage the limited resources of the Old World’s medicine to mend bodies shattered by the void and minds broken by the silence of the Night.

## Subclasses

### The Clinician

The Clinician is a master of the physical vessel. They specialize in trauma surgery, the treatment of radiation sickness, and the eradication of exotic diseases found in derelict stations. They are experts at "making do" with improvised tools, performing life-saving operations in the cramped, dirty quarters of a moving ship.

### The Counselor

The Counselor heals the "ghost in the machine." In a society suffering from collective PTSD and deep depression, they provide the mental fortitude necessary to keep going. Their deep understanding of the human psyche makes them expert negotiators and interrogators; they can read a person's lies as easily as a Clinician reads a fever.

## Core Ability

**Triage:** A Medic can instantly assess the "breaking point" of a person—whether it is physical (how many more hits they can take) or mental (how close they are to a breakdown or lying).
