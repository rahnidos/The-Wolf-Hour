# The Operator
In an age where most people treat computers as inscrutable black boxes or sleeping gods, the Operator sees them for what they are: logic, electricity, and opportunity. They are the masters of the digital ghost-world that still hums beneath the surface of the ruins.

## Subclasses

### The Hacker

The Hacker is a specialist in software intrusion and data manipulation. They can bypass ancient firewalls, crack encrypted ship logs, and seize control of remote systems. In the Wolf Hour, a Hacker doesn't just steal data-they can turn a station’s own security against it or shut down a pursuing ship’s engines with a few keystrokes.

### The Hardware Specialist

The Hardware Specialist focuses on the physical side of computing. They are the ones who can swap out burnt-out processors, repair fractured mainframes, and physically "reboot" long-dormant AI cores. While the Hacker fights the software, the Specialist ensures the machine has the power and stability to run in the first place.

## Core Ability

**System Override:** An Operator can attempt to force a connection with any electronic device, even those without an obvious interface, gaining a brief window of control or a diagnostic readout of the machine's state.
