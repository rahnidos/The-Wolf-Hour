# The Techwright

In the Wolf Hour, a Techwright is more than an engineer; they are a guardian of the remaining light. They understand the language of the Old World machines and possess the rare ability to coax life out of cold, rusted iron.


## Subclasses

### The Mechanic

The Mechanic deals with the physical, the heavy, and the tangible. They are masters of hydraulics, airlocks, and the massive engines that propel humanity through the void. They focus on structural integrity, life support systems, and the brute force of machinery.

### The Roboticist

The Roboticist works with the "nerves" of the machine. They deal with the flickering remnants of electronics, sensor arrays, and the dangerous, dormant logic of ancient automata. They are the only ones capable of interfacing with damaged systems or repairing corrupted circuitry.

## Core Ability

**Scrapper’s Intuition:** Once per session, a Techwright can identify the exact point of failure in any machine or structure, or find a way to jury-rig a broken device using only the scrap at hand.
