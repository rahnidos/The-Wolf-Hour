# The Salvager

The Old World did not leave a manual on how to survive its collapse. The Salvager is the one who goes into the skeletal remains of cities and stations to find the fuel, the parts, and the truths that keep the Wolf Hour from turning back into the Night.

## Subclasses

### The Stalker

The Stalker is a specialist in physical infiltration and survival in high-risk environments. They are experts at navigating the "Shadow-Scars," bypassing ancient security systems, and identifying which pieces of scrap are worth their weight in fuel. They know where to step to avoid a hull-breach and how to move silently through halls haunted by feral machines.

### The Archivist

The Archivist is a hunter of information. They don't just find objects; they find meaning. They can interface with corrupted data-slates, translate forgotten programming languages, and piece together the history of a ruin from a few scraps of paper or digital fragments. To an Archivist, knowledge is the most valuable resource in the galaxy.

### The Survivalist

The Survivalist is a master of the "green ruins" and feral worlds. While others focus on dead stations, the Survivalist knows how to breathe the air of untamed planets, which local flora is edible, and how to track or avoid the mutated fauna that has claimed the Old World’s colonies. They are experts at finding shelter and staying alive when technology fails completely.
## Core Ability

**Scavenger's Eye:** A Salvager can spend a moment examining a ruin or a piece of junk to determine its original purpose, its current value, and any immediate dangers associated with it.
