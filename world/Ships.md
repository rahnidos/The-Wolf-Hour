# Ships

The only means of travel in the universe are spaceships. Currently, no new ships are produced; there are no functioning shipyards, nor is there the knowledge or technology to build them. The only flying ships are those that have been found, and they are not always fully functional.

Based on size and function, ships are divided into the following types:

- [Scout](app://obsidian.md/Scout.md)
- Medium
- Heavy
- Transport / Cruise Liner
- Carrier
- Mothership

Ships larger than the Heavy type are unable to land on planets or moons; they are equipped with landers. Even Heavy ships do not always handle atmospheric flight well.

## Three Flight Modes

There are three primary models of travel: short-range flight, intra-system movement, and long-distance travel beyond the star system.

**Short-range flight** is powered by traditional jet engines. These are low-speed trips, mostly within the vicinity of a planet and its satellites. All ships are capable of this, though not all can land. This category includes landing maneuvers and docking. The required skill is [Piloting](app://obsidian.md/Piloting.md).

For further travel, a ship must be equipped with a **Jump Drive**. This drive operates in two modes: Superluminal and Hyperspace.

**Superluminal Mode** is used for traveling within a star system. It allows acceleration to approximately fifty times the speed of light. The ship requires time for this, as it must begin braking maneuvers roughly halfway to the destination. To enter superluminal mode, a ship must be at least 5 km away from any large mass. Preparation takes about 5 minutes. This requires the [Piloting](app://obsidian.md/Piloting.md) skill.

**Hyperspace Mode** allows jumps between systems. It has several limitations:

- **Jump Distance:** Depends on ship mass and engine size. Jumps range from a few to a maximum of 70 light-years.
- **Jump Time:** A single jump lasts a few seconds. If the ship was already in superluminal mode, it is automatically prepared. If not, preparation takes about 5 minutes.
- **Targeting Stars:** Jumps are always made "to a star." The exit point is always near the system's star (the heaviest one in multi-star systems). Certain stars (like supernovas) cannot be jumped to.
- **Jump Computer:** Before the Catastrophe, AIs calculated jump parameters instantly. Now, most AIs are broken or untrusted. Therefore, ships only jump to coordinates already stored in the jump computer. This data is traded among travelers. Entering new coordinates or calculating multi-jumps requires [Astrogation](app://obsidian.md/Astrogation.md).
- **Fuel:** Jumps consume large amounts of fuel depending on ship size. A ship's range is usually measured in light-years per full tank.

## Fuel and Refueling

Ships may be equipped with small refineries that produce fuel from hydrogen (collected near gas giants) or water. If a refinery works perfectly—which is rare—filling the tanks takes about five hours.

Ships without refineries must use planetary facilities or scavenge fuel from abandoned orbital stations and service outposts on asteroids and moons.
