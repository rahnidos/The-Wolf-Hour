# The Old World

In the distant future, humanity reached the technical pinnacle of its capabilities. It understood the universe and its laws well enough to use them to achieve all realistic goals. People had everything that philosophers of the time deemed necessary for happiness. Despite this, people were quite far from that happiness, perhaps precisely because of their high awareness, knowledge, and intelligence.

## Territory
For many years, humans colonized nearby planets and entire star systems. Humanity focused mainly on a bubble area with a radius of about 300 light-years. The Solar System remained the center of the bubble. Within the bubble, there were about 20,000 star systems, of which 15,000 were colonized and inhabited.

The core of the Bubble—specifically the Solar System and surrounding sectors—was a marvel of cosmic engineering. Massive **megastructures** defined this region: Dyson swarms captured the full output of stars, orbital elevators stretched from planetary surfaces to shimmering space docks, and arcologies housed billions in perfect climate-controlled comfort. The closer one traveled to the center, the more the environment felt entirely synthetic and optimized.

## Technology
Access to the resources of the entire universe, a great diversity of worlds, and near-infinite energy through fusion and vacuum-point extraction led to a post-scarcity economy.

Artificial Intelligence was the silent backbone of existence. These advanced entities managed 99% of human logistics, from planetary climate control to the microscopic distribution of resources. They were not merely tools, but perfect companions that anticipated human needs before they were even voiced, ensuring that the gears of civilization turned without friction or error.

While physical travel was handled by Fold Drives, information was even more fluid. A near-instantaneous synchronization network allowed the "Omni-Net" to function across the Bubble. Data, memories, and collective knowledge were shared at the speed of thought, making isolation a choice rather than a condition.

## Biology and Transhumanism

Biological aging was solved for the elite, making death an "optional inconvenience." However, the reach of biological science went much further. **Cyborgization** was a standard path for many; the integration of neural links and synthetic enhancements allowed humans to interface directly with the AI and the Omni-Net.

Genetic sculpting, cloning, and the "printing" of organic tissues meant that the human form was highly malleable. Whether through sleek cybernetic limbs or profound biological modifications, people could adapt their bodies to suit any environment or aesthetic desire, pushing the boundaries of what it meant to be "human."
## Politics
The Bubble was a vast, decentralized patchwork of ideologies. Its sheer size allowed radically different systems to coexist, often in a state of cold war or uneasy trade:

- **Corporate Domains:** Systems owned entirely by mega-corporations. Here, laws were replaced by employment contracts, and "citizens" were merely assets.
- **The Pilots' Union:** A massive collective built on the ideals of "The Brotherhood." They practiced a form of space-faring communalism, prioritizing mutual aid and egalitarian resource sharing.
- **The Neo-Feudal Empire:** A centralized power ruled by a strict hierarchy of nobility. It valued honor, status, and a rigid social order, with systems granted as fiefdoms to loyal Lords.
- **The Independent Fringe:** Thousands of isolated stations and outposts that experimented with everything from pure anarchy to religious theocracies.
