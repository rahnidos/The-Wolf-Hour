# Introduction

This is not an era of grand empires, nor is it the total darkness of the end. It is the **Wolf Hour**—the fragile, dangerous moment just before the first light of a new dawn.

After centuries of **The Night**, during which humanity hid in the shadows from terrors that nearly extinguished all life, the gates to the stars are finally creaking open. Outside lies the "Old World"—a silent graveyard of a god-like civilization, filled with wonders of a forgotten age and the lingering horrors that brought it down.

In this world, you might find yourself as a **pioneer** charting the forgotten reaches or a **scavenger** hunting for the powerful artifacts of the past. You could be the first to bridge the gap between isolated colonies or an explorer seeking the truth behind the cataclysm. This is an age for those brave enough to step out of the dark, to reclaim the ruins, and to decide if humanity will rise again or vanish forever in the morning mist.
