The Wolf Hour is still too early for humanity to have fully recovered. Human settlements are small outposts; no new empires, kingdoms, inter-system unions, or even the simplest guilds have yet emerged. The world during the Wolf Hour is still filled with evil and darkness. People fear drawing attention to themselves, dreading the gaze of planet-devouring monsters, rogue artificial intelligences, or even common thieves and scavengers.

Depending on the conditions they find themselves in, people mostly form a few types of communities:

- _Small colonies gathered around still-functioning terraformers._
- _People nesting in abandoned orbital stations and motherships._

Among the worlds where humanity slowly flickers back to life, some are entirely unaware of the catastrophe or the might of past civilizations. These groups, born from a handful of survivors, form small feudal or communal agricultural hubs focused on survival. Typically, these outposts are found on Earth-like planets where survival is possible without knowledge of Old World technology.

They generally dislike guests; it is also often the case that once discovered, these groups are robbed or even slaughtered by space-faring scavengers.
