# The Galaxy Today

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy. This vast region of the universe can accommodate any possible campaign. In reality, however, the game takes place among the ruins of the [Old World](app://obsidian.md/Old%20World.md). Before the catastrophe, the colonized bubble consisted of approximately fifteen thousand star systems. Nevertheless, nothing prevents you from moving the game beyond the old bubble if you feel the need.

Before the fall, humanity inhabited a massive expanse. Technology allowed for the colonization of not only Earth-like planets but essentially any celestial body capable of supporting a residential habitat. Space was also filled with orbital stations and massive residential ships. The remains of all this are still accessible today.

The Solar System, the cradle of humanity, was the central hub of the bubble. The bubble itself was approximately five hundred light-years in diameter. Of course, population density was not uniform. The center of the bubble was dense with civilization, while the periphery was filled with small mining settlements or fully automated factories. However, it was in the center that the catastrophe began, and the center suffered the most destruction. Today, only the bravest venture there. [Places](app://obsidian.md/Civilization.md) where humanity might attempt to rebuild are located on the edges of the bubble, separated by many light-years of empty space. They are linked only by the rare visits of wandering traders, swindlers, or preachers roaming the galaxy.
