# Bastion Domain Cards

## Hardened Shell
**Level 1 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 0**
Your passive Armor Score increases by +1. You may spend 1 Hope to ignore the *Shaken* condition when you take damage.
## Cover Ally
**Level 1 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 0**
When an adjacent ally is attacked, you may spend 1 Stress to become the target of that attack instead. Use your own Evasion and Armor.
## Steady Vitals
**Level 1 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 0**
Once per long rest, when you would be reduced to 0 HP, you instead drop to 1 HP.
## Reactive Shielding
**Level 2 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
When you take damage, you can spend 1 Hope to gain a temporary +2 to your Armor Score against the next attack targeting you in this encounter.
## Brace for Impact
**Level 2 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
Spend 1 Stress to gain Advantage on any roll made to resist being moved, knocked prone, or stunned.
## Protector's Stance
**Level 2 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
As long as you are standing within close range of an ally, that ally gains +1 to their Evasion.
## Reinforced Plating
**Level 3 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
Add your Proficiency bonus to your physical Damage Thresholds.
## Interpose
**Level 3 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
When an enemy moves toward an ally within your movement range, you may spend 1 Hope to immediately move and intercept them, stopping their movement adjacent to you.
## Iron Will
**Level 3 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
You can spend 1 Hope to use your Strength or Constitution modifier instead of your Instinct or Presence when making a roll to resist mental influence or fear.
## Energy Barrier
**Level 4 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Hope. You create a stationary energy field in a small area. Any ally inside the area gains +3 Armor Score against ranged attacks. Lasts until the end of the scene or until you move.
## Hold the Line
**Level 4 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Enemies cannot move through the space adjacent to you without your permission. If they try, spend 1 Stress to make a free melee attack; on a hit, their movement ends immediately.
## Unyielding Resolve
**Level 4 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Whenever you spend a point of Armor to reduce damage, roll a d6. On a 5-6, you do not mark the Armor slot (it remains usable).
## Kinetic Absorption
**Level 5 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
When you take damage that exceeds your Major Threshold, gain 1 Hope.
## Fortress Protocol
**Level 5 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Stress. For the rest of the encounter, you treat all "Minor" damage hits as "Zero" damage hits, but your movement is halved.
## Sentinel's Gaze
**Level 5 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
You cannot be surprised, and enemies do not gain Advantage when attacking you from hiding or flanking.
## Aegis Aura
**Level 6 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Hope. For the next three rounds, all allies within far range of you may use your Armor Score instead of their own.
## System Redundancy
**Level 6 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
You gain an additional Armor slot. Additionally, repairing your armor during a short rest costs no resources.
## Bastion of Hope
**Level 7 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Whenever you use an Armor slot to protect an ally, both you and that ally gain 1 Hope.
## Phalanx Maneuver
**Level 7 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Hope. You and up to three allies within close range can move together as a single unit. As long as you stay adjacent, everyone in the group gains +2 Armor Score.
## Juggernaut Momentum
**Level 8 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
While at full HP, your attacks deal an additional damage die. While below half HP, you gain +2 to all Damage Thresholds.
## Unbreakable Frame
**Level 8 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
Spend 3 Hope. You become immune to all damage and conditions until the start of your next turn.
## Colossal Presence
**Level 9 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
You count as one size larger for the purposes of combat. Your reach increases, and you can provide full cover to up to two allies standing behind you.
## Reflective Surface
**Level 9 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
When an enemy misses a ranged attack against you, you can spend 1 Stress to redirect the attack back at them using their original roll.
## Immortal Guard
**Level 10 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
Spend 5 Hope. For the duration of the scene, you cannot be reduced below 1 HP by any means.
## Shelter from the Night
**Level 10 Bastion Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
Once per session, you can create a dome of pure light. No creature of the Night or Void can enter this dome, and all allies inside are instantly cleared of all Stress.
