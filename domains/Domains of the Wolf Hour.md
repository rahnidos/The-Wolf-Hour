
# Domains of the Wolf Hour
### Force

Mastery of direct combat, firearms, and pure physical dominance on the battlefield.

### Bastion

Focuses on defense, physical hardening, and serving as a shield for allies against the horrors of the Night.

### Survival

Stealth, tracking, wilderness survival, and the art of remaining undetected by the predators of the dark.

### Void

Psionics, rituals, investigating the darkness, and sensing supernatural entities born from the Night.

### Radiance

Inspiration, leadership, diplomacy, and the ability to bring hope to the darkest corners of the galaxy.

### Scraps

Improvisation, trading, scavenging resources, and the ability to craft functional gear from junk.

### Mechanics

Building and repairing devices, operating heavy machinery, robotics, and physical engineering.

### Data

Hacking, decryption, AI manipulation, cyber-warfare, and the analysis of complex information systems.

### Vector

Piloting, astrogation, operating long-range sensors, and executing vehicle maneuvers in vacuum or atmosphere.
