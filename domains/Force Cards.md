## Point Blank
**Level 1 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 0**
When making a ranged attack within close range, you may spend 1 Hope to add an additional damage die to your roll.
## Brutal Strike
**Level 1 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 0**
Your melee attacks deal +2 damage. Once per combat, you can spend 1 Stress to knock a standard-sized enemy prone on a successful hit.
## Warning Shot
**Level 1 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 0**
Spend 1 Hope. Make a ranged attack roll against an enemy’s Fear DC. On a success, the target is Restrained by fear until the start of your next turn.
## Adrenaline Rush
**Level 2 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
Spend 1 Hope to take an additional move action or to stand up from prone without using an action.
## Shield Bash
**Level 2 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
If you are using a shield or cover, spend 1 Stress to shove an adjacent enemy. They take physical damage equal to your Proficiency and are pushed back.
## Burst Fire
**Level 2 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
Spend 2 Hope. Choose a small area. Make a single ranged attack roll and apply it to all enemies within that area. Deal half damage on a miss.
## Cleave
**Level 3 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
When you reduce an enemy to 0 HP with a melee attack, you can immediately make another melee attack against a different adjacent target.
## Unstoppable Charge
**Level 3 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
Move up to your full distance in a straight line. Any enemies in your path must succeed on a Strength roll or be knocked aside. You may spend 1 Hope to make a melee attack at the end with Advantage.
## Precision Kill
**Level 3 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 1**
When you roll a critical success, you may choose to deal maximum damage instead of rolling, or spend 1 Hope to inflict a Bleeding condition (1 Stress per turn).
## Suppressive Fire
**Level 4 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 1 Hope. Until your next turn, any enemy in a chosen zone that moves or attacks takes Stress equal to your Proficiency.
## Executioner
**Level 4 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
You deal an extra damage die against any target that is currently below half their maximum HP.
## Overwhelming Power
**Level 4 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Stress. Your next successful hit automatically stuns a standard or large enemy until the end of their next turn.
## Weapon Master
**Level 5 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Choose one weapon type (e.g., Heavy Rifles). You gain Advantage on all attack rolls with that weapon.
## Sunder Armor
**Level 5 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 1 Hope on a hit. The target’s Armor Score is permanently reduced by 2 for the remainder of the encounter (minimum 0).
## Whirlwind of Lead
**Level 5 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Once per long rest, spend 3 Hope. You fire in a 360-degree arc. All enemies within far range must make a secondary Evasion roll or take massive damage.
## Juggernaut
**Level 6 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
While in combat, you gain +2 to your Armor Score. You cannot be moved against your will by enemies of your size or smaller.
## Point of Impact
**Level 6 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Hope. Your next attack ignores all Damage Thresholds, dealing direct damage to the target's HP.
## Battle Cry
**Level 7 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
Spend 2 Hope. You and all allies within close range gain Advantage on their next Force or Fear roll.
## Collateral Damage
**Level 7 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 2**
When you miss an attack, you can spend 1 Stress to deal half damage anyway as you destroy the environment around the target.
## Relentless Assault
**Level 8 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
If your attack hits, you may spend 1 Hope to immediately make another attack against the same target. You can repeat this as long as you have Hope and continue to hit.
## Deafening Blast
**Level 8 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
Spend 2 Stress. Your attack creates a kinetic shockwave. All nearby targets are pushed back and cannot hear for the next minute.
## One-Man Army
**Level 9 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
When engaged with three or more enemies, you gain +3 to all attack rolls and +2 to all Damage Thresholds.
## Titan’s Grip
**Level 9 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
You can wield two-handed weapons in one hand without penalty. If wielding with two hands, add two extra damage dice.
## Avatar of Destruction
**Level 10 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
Spend 5 Hope. For the next three turns, all your successful attacks are treated as critical hits.
## World-Ender Shot
**Level 10 Force Ability**
**Recall cost 3**
Once per session, spend all remaining Hope (min 3) to make a single ranged attack that deals triple damage and vaporizes any standard enemy instantly.
