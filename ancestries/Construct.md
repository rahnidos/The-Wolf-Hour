# Construct
You are a human consciousness housed within a mechanical shell. While your mind is a relic of the biological world, the trauma of the Night or the process of digital transfer has scrubbed your memories clean. You possess no more knowledge of the Old World than any other survivor, your past hidden behind a wall of static. Your body, however, is a product of engineering, designed to endure where flesh would fail.

## Features

### Vessel Configuration

Your consciousness is installed in a specific chassis. Work with your GM to define the shape and size of your construct (e.g., Humanoid, Multi-legged, or Small Drone). Based on your form, you gain one of the following benefits:

- **Integrated Mobility:** If you have wings, rotors, or many legs, you gain a unique movement type (Flight, Climbing, or increased Speed).
- **Specialized Sensors/Tools:** If your form is utility-focused, you have a built-in tool or sensor suite that grants Advantage on specific technical or perception checks.
- **Reinforced Frame:** If your form is heavy or industrial, you gain +1 to your Armor Score.

### Synthetic Existence

Your mechanical body does not require air, food, or sleep, and you are immune to biological poisons and diseases. However, you cannot be healed by traditional medicine. You must be repaired using scrap and tools (making a relevant technical check during a rest), or you can spend a **Hope** to bypass a system failure and instantly restore a portion of your integrity.
