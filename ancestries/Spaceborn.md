# Spaceborn
Descendants of those who spent generations aboard space stations, drifting colony ships, or hollowed-out asteroids. Their bodies have subtly shifted to match the cold efficiency of the void.

### Void Adaptation

Your lineage has evolved to thrive in low-gravity or artificial environments. Choose one of the following physical modifications:

- **Prehensile Feet:** You can use your feet to grip tools or surfaces as if they were hands, granting advantage on checks to climb or stay anchored in zero-G.
- **Low-Light Vision:** Your eyes have adapted to the dim, flickering lights of dying stations. You can see clearly in near-total darkness.
- **Enhanced Equilibrium:** Your inner ear is immune to disorientation. You cannot be knocked prone or made dizzy by rapid rotation or zero-G movement.

### Station Sense
You have an innate understanding of artificial structures. When you are inside a ship, station, or vault, you have Advantage on intuition or perception checks to find maintenance hatches, hidden wiring, or structural weaknesses.
