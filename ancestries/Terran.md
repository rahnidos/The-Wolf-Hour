# Terran
Descendants of those who survived on Earth-like worlds, Terrans are the most numerous and widespread ancestry in the known sectors. They are defined by their remarkable versatility and the stubborn refusal to let the human flame go out.

## Features

### Adaptive Talent

Once per long rest, when you fail a roll using a skill you are not proficient in, you can reroll it. You must take the new result.

### Unyielding Resolve

When you would take Stress, you can choose to spend a Hope to ignore it instead. You can do this once per short rest.
