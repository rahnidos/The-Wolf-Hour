# Cryonaut
You are a relic of the Old World, awakened from a cryochamber with no memory of your past. While the Night claimed your memories, the long sleep in the freezing dark has left you with strange, lingering echoes of what was and what lurks in the shadows.

## Features

### Echoes of the Past

Your amnesia is punctuated by sudden, sharp flashes of clarity. You can spend a **Hope** to recall a specific fragment of information about a pre-Night object, location, or technological system. This grants you **Advantage** on a check to operate, repair, or navigate it, or provides a crucial piece of lore about its original purpose. This cannot be used to recreate complex technology from scratch.

### Void-Touched Psionics

The long sleep through the Night has altered your brainwaves. Choose one of the following abilities:

- **The Silent Whisper:** You can telepathically communicate with any creature you can see. This "whisper" is undetectable by conventional means and allows you to send brief messages without speaking aloud.
- **Night-Sense:** You can feel the psychic "residue" of the Night. You gain **Advantage** on checks to sense the presence, general direction, or immediate hostile intentions of monsters and entities born from the Night.
