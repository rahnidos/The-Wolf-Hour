# Extremis
Descendants of survivors who refused to die on worlds that tried to kill them. Whether from high-gravity moons, acid-rain swamps, or tidally locked hellscapes, their bodies have been forged by extremity.

## Features

### Harsh Evolution

Your ancestors’ struggle has left a permanent mark on your physiology. Choose one of the following adaptations:

- **Dense Musculature:** Forged by high gravity, your strength is legendary. You can carry twice the normal limit and gain Advantage on checks to lift, push, or break heavy objects.
- **Reinforced Dermis:** Your skin is thick and leathery, or perhaps covered in fine scales. You gain a +1 bonus to your Armor Score.
- **Filtered Metabolism:** Your body is remarkably efficient at processing toxins. You have Advantage on rolls to resist poisons, radiation, or the effects of a corrosive atmosphere.
### Environmental Hardening

When you take damage from an environmental source (such as extreme heat, cold, acid, or pressure), you may spend a Hope to reduce that damage by half.
